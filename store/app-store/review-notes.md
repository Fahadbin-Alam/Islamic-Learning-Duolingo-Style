# App Review notes

Paste into App Store Connect → the version page → App Review Information → Notes. Fill in the contact name, phone and email above the notes.

```
Sira Path teaches Islamic knowledge (Quran, seerah, the Prophets, the companions, prayer and manners) through short quiz lessons.

No sign-in is required. To review:
1. Open the app and start from Foundation, or pick another topic.
2. Tap the next lesson on the path, answer the questions and finish the lesson summary.
3. Open the shop to see Sira Path Premium (auto-renewing, monthly or yearly), heart packs and gems. Purchases can be restored from the shop.

Creating an account is optional and only saves progress across devices. You can create one from the account screen with any email address and password. Accounts can be deleted in the app from Profile > Account > Delete account.

Free users see AdMob banner ads, an occasional ad between lessons (never inside a lesson) and optional rewarded ads for an extra heart. Ads are limited to the G content rating. Premium removes ads.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device; the app does not use push notifications.
```

Sign-in information: leave "Sign-in required" unticked, since the app works without an account. If App Review asks for an account anyway, create one on the production backend and add it here.
