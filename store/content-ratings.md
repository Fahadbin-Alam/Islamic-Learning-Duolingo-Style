# Content rating answers

Both stores ask about the content of the app, not about who it is for. Sira Path teaches religious knowledge in text quizzes. Historical lessons mention events such as the battle of Uhud and companions who were martyred, in short text without description of injury. The marriage topic covers choosing a spouse well and mercy in the home, with no sexual content. Reward chests are earned by learning, cannot be bought, and are not gambling.

Form wording changes from time to time. If a question is not listed here, answer it from the description above, and when unsure pick the stricter answer.

## Google Play (IARC questionnaire)

Play Console → Policy and programs → App content → Content rating.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Email address for the rating certificate | Your support email |
| Category | **Reference, News, or Educational** |
| Violence: does the app contain violence, blood or gore? | **No**. Historical events are mentioned in brief text only, with no depictions |
| Fear: frightening or horror content? | No |
| Sexuality: nudity, sexual content or references? | No |
| Language: profanity or crude humour? | No |
| Controlled substances: drugs, alcohol or tobacco? | No (rulings may mention that alcohol is forbidden, which is not depiction or promotion) |
| Gambling: real or simulated gambling? | No |
| Does the app let users interact or exchange content with each other? | **No**. Learners can follow each other by account email and see each other's display name, XP and streak on a weekly leaderboard; there is no chat, messaging or user-posted content |
| Does the app share the user's current physical location with other users? | No |
| Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods? | **Yes** (subscription, heart packs, gems) |
| Does the app contain randomised items offered for purchase (loot boxes)? | No |
| Is the app a web browser or search engine? | No |
| Does the app contain content generated by users? | No |

Expected result: **ESRB Everyone, PEGI 3, USK 0, IARC 3+**, with the "In-App Purchases" notice. Ads are declared separately and show as "Contains ads" on the listing.
