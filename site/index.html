Learn Islam a little every day

Muslimo is a free-to-download app for Android and iPhone that teaches Islamic knowledge through short, game-like lessons: the basics of iman, wudu and salah, the Quran and tafsir by surah, the lives of the Prophets, the Sahabah, hadith and good manners. Keep a streak, earn XP and review what you missed.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can start learning straight away. An account saves your progress so you can keep it on a new phone.

How do I restore premium on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with, open the shop in Muslimo and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel premium?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or through Google Play.

I found a mistake in a lesson.

JazakAllahu khayran for telling us. Email the lesson name and what looks wrong, with a source if you have one, and we will review it.

How do I turn off reminders?

Change reminder settings in the app, or turn off notifications for Muslimo in your phone's settings.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on the account deletion page.
