Delete your Muslimo account

Muslimo is made by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Muslimo and sign in.
  2. Open your profile and tap Settings.
  3. Scroll down, tap Delete account and confirm.

Delete without the app

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Muslimo account". We confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days. If you write from a different address, we will ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

Subscriptions are separate. Deleting your account does not cancel a premium subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store account settings or in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, password hash and account settings
  • Your learning progress, XP, streaks, gems, hearts and rewards
  • Your answers, placement results and review history
  • Your Crew list and any pending Crew requests, and you are removed from other learners' crews
  • Your subscription status stored with your account, sign-in tokens and app usage events

Deleting in the app removes your account from our servers straight away and signs you out on that device. Email requests are completed within 30 days. Backups are overwritten within a further 30 days.

What may be kept

  • Purchase and billing records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat, which they keep under their own policies and tax laws. Email us if you also want us to ask RevenueCat to delete your purchase history.
  • Information we must keep by law, or to resolve a dispute or prevent fraud, for only as long as needed.

Progress saved on your device without an account is removed when you uninstall the app.
