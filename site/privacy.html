Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Sira Path is an Islamic learning app for Android and iOS. It is made by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

The short version. You can learn without an account. If you create one, we store your name, email and learning progress so it is saved across devices. Free learners see ads from Google AdMob. Children, teens and anyone who has not told us their age only see non-personalised ads. Purchases go through Apple or Google and RevenueCat; we never see your card details. We do not sell your personal information for money, and you can delete your account at any time.

If you use the app without an account

Your progress, settings and streaks are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.

Your age group

Before any ad is shown, the app asks once how old you are (9 or under, 10 to 12, 13 to 17, or 18 or older). Nothing is chosen for you. The answer is stored only on your device and is not sent to us; it only decides which ads the app may show, as described below.

Information collected by our partners

Advertising (free learners only). We show ads using Google AdMob. AdMob collects your IP address (which can show your approximate location), information about your device, and how you interact with ads, so that it can show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. What else it may use depends on your age group:

  • 12 or under, a Kid account, or no age given yet: ads are requested as directed to children under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). They are not personalised, and AdMob does not use the advertising identifier to build a profile.
  • 13 to 17: ads are requested as for users under the age of consent. They are not personalised.
  • 18 or older: on Android, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show personalised ads, but only after you agree in Google's consent message where the law requires one (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states). On iPhone, ads are never personalised, because the app does not ask to track you.

Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences, and never appear inside a lesson or when the app opens. Premium members do not see ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases. Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to check purchases and restore them on your other devices. RevenueCat receives a purchase id (your account id if you are signed in, otherwise a random id), the products you bought, and purchase dates. We never see your payment card or bank details. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Information that stays on your device

Daily reminders and Islamic reminders are scheduled by your phone and are not sent through our servers. We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the app: save progress, run lessons, review, streaks, rewards and premium features.
  • To personalise learning, such as choosing question difficulty and review.
  • To keep accounts secure and prevent abuse and fraud.
  • To show ads to free learners and to process purchases.
  • To fix problems and improve lessons, using learning statistics.
  • To reply when you contact us, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not use your answers or progress to build advertising profiles, and we do not send them to advertisers.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We use your information to perform our agreement with you (providing your account, progress and purchases), for our legitimate interests (keeping the app secure, improving lessons), with your consent (personalised ads where consent is required), and to meet legal obligations.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only with:

  • Service providers that host our servers and database and that process data for us under contract.
  • Google AdMob for ads shown to free learners, as described above.
  • Other learners who follow you, who see your display name, XP, streak and when you last learned, as described above.
  • Apple, Google and RevenueCat to process and verify purchases.
  • Authorities when required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of our users or others.
  • A new owner if the app is transferred, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

Under some US state laws, such as California's, letting an ad network use your advertising identifier to show you personalised ads can count as "sharing" for advertising. This can only happen for adults on Android. In US states with these laws, the app shows Google's privacy message, where you can opt out, and you can also turn on "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" in your phone's settings. We never sell or share information about learners under 18 this way. Premium removes ads completely.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your account information while your account is open. Sign-in tokens expire after 30 days. When you delete your account, we delete your account, progress, answers, review history, the people you follow, subscription status and usage events from our systems within 30 days, apart from backups, which are overwritten within a further 30 days. Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat follow their own retention rules and tax laws.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account.
  • Change your language, reminder and sound settings in the app.
  • Turn off notifications in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings, or remove ads with premium.
  • Delete your account by following the steps on our account deletion page.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address on your account. We reply within 30 days and will not treat you differently for using your rights. If you are in the EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Sira Path is made for learners of all ages, from young children to adults, and for families learning together.

  • Learning without an account. A child can learn without an account. Their progress stays on the device and we do not receive it.
  • Accounts. Creating an account, buying something and adding people to a Crew are behind a grown-up check. A parent or guardian should create any account for a child under 13, using the parent's own email address, and can mark it as a Kid account in Settings. We use a child's account information only to save their progress and run the app, never for advertising.
  • Ads. Children see only non-personalised, child-directed ads rated for general audiences, as described in section 1. The age answer stays on the device.
  • Your rights as a parent. You can review or delete your child's account information, or stop us collecting more, by emailing {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or deleting the account in the app.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information without a parent's involvement, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

9. International transfers

Our servers and partners may be in countries other than yours, including the United States. When we transfer information from the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

10. Links to other websites

Lessons link to sources such as Quran.com, Sunnah.com, YouTube and Yaqeen Institute. Those sites open outside the app and have their own privacy policies.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
