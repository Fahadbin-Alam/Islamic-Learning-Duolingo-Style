# Abu Bakr as-Siddiq

Abu Bakr was the closest friend of the Prophet ﷺ and the first of the [[Rightly Guided Caliphs]].

## Accepting Islam

Abu Bakr accepted Islam without hesitation when the Prophet ﷺ first invited him. He then brought others to Islam, including ==Uthman ibn Affan== and Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf. #early-muslims

Q: Who was among the people Abu Bakr brought to Islam?
- [x] Uthman ibn Affan
- [ ] Abu Jahl
- [ ] Abu Lahab
Explanation: Abu Bakr's dawah brought several of the earliest Muslims, Uthman among them.

What title is Abu Bakr known by?:: As-Siddiq, the truthful affirmer

## The Hijrah

Abu Bakr accompanied the Prophet ﷺ on the Hijrah to Madinah, and they hid together in the cave of Thawr. Allah refers to this moment in [Surah at-Tawbah 9:40](https://quran.com/9/40).

Q: Where did the Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr hide during the Hijrah?
A: The cave of Thawr

Which surah mentions the two in the cave?:: Surah at-Tawbah

T/F: Abu Bakr stayed behind in Makkah during the Hijrah.
A: False
> He travelled with the Prophet ﷺ and was his companion in the cave.

### Key takeaway
True friendship means standing with the truth when it is costly.

## Leading the Ummah

After the Prophet ﷺ passed away, Abu Bakr became the first caliph. He held the Muslims together and kept the zakat and the religion as the Prophet ﷺ left them.

Who was the first caliph after the Prophet ﷺ?:: Abu Bakr as-Siddiq
