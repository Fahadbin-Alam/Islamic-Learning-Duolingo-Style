## Speak good or stay silent

The Prophet ﷺ taught that whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day should speak good or remain silent. See https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6018

Q: What should a believer do if they cannot say something good?
- [ ] Say it anyway
- [x] Remain silent
- [ ] Say it quietly

## Kind words to people

Allah commands the believers to speak to people with good words in Surah al-Baqarah. https://quran.com/2/83

True or false: Allah commands speaking good words to people.
A: True

## Avoiding backbiting

Backbiting means mentioning your brother in a way he would dislike, even if it is true.
