# Lesson photo credits

## Drawings of how places looked long ago

Makkah, the Ka'bah, Madinah, Mount an-Nur (Hira), Mount Uhud, Zamzam, Safa and Marwah, Arafah, Mina, Muzdalifah,
Ta'if and Jerusalem are our own drawings (`scripts/lesson-pictures/draw.mjs`), not photos: the photos of today show
towers, cranes, lamps, roads and crowds that were not there in the early years of Islam. They follow what the seerah
books describe (a dry valley of dark mountains with flat-roofed houses round the open space of the Ka'bah; Madinah's
date-palm groves and black lava fields with Uhud to the north). Only places: never a person. Re-draw with
`node scripts/lesson-pictures/draw.mjs`.
