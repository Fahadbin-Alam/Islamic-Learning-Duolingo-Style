# Privacy Policy Draft

Muslimo respects your privacy and only collects the information needed to run the learning experience, save progress, and support accounts.

## Information We Collect

- account information such as name, email address, and sign-in provider
- lesson progress, XP, streaks, mastery, quiz scores, and review history (saved on our server for account holders, so it returns on a new phone)
- question attempts and learning activity
- subscription and entitlement state when premium features are enabled
- optional social connection data that you choose to add

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- create and secure your account
- save your learning progress across sessions
- personalize lessons, review, and recommendations
- support streaks, reminders, and premium access
- improve the product and monitor reliability

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with service providers that help run authentication, hosting, analytics, notifications, or billing.

## Advertising and Purchases

Free learners see ads served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change that choice later. Premium members do not see ads.

When you tap Listen on an ayah, the app downloads that ayah's recitation from the Islamic Network audio server (alquran.cloud), which sees your IP address like any website. Nothing about you or your progress is sent.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase id (or your account id if you are signed in) and your purchase history so premium can be restored on your other devices. We never see your card details.

## Your Choices

You can:

- use core learning without creating an account where supported
- update account information
- log out of the app
- request account deletion when production account management is finalized

## Security

We use reasonable measures to protect account and progress data, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

## Children

Parents and guardians may use connected accountability features where available. Children should use the app with supervision appropriate to their age and local laws.
